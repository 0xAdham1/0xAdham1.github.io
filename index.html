(05) Responsible disclosure

Breaking things.
Responsibly.

Testing belongs inside the program's scope. A useful disclosure is careful, reproducible, and written to help the owner remediate, not to expose people to unnecessary risk.

My goal is simple: find the bug, explain the root cause, and leave the target stronger than it was before.
