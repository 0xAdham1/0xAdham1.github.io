## CVEs

Vulnerabilities I found and disclosed, each reported to the vendor before
publication and documented in a full writeup.

### Krayin CRM

A white-box review of [Krayin CRM](https://github.com/krayin/laravel-crm)
(`krayin/laravel-crm`) turned up three issues, all fixed in **2.2.7**:

- [CVE-2026-112286](https://nvd.nist.gov/vuln/detail/CVE-2026-112286) — Quote module IDOR, cross-user read (CWE-639)
- [CVE-2026-112287](https://nvd.nist.gov/vuln/detail/CVE-2026-112287) — Mail module BOLA on the write path (CWE-639)
- [CVE-2026-112288](https://nvd.nist.gov/vuln/detail/CVE-2026-112288) — Webhook SSRF filter bypass via DNS rebinding (CWE-918 / CWE-367)

Writeup: [A Patch Is Not a Fix: Chaining Krayin CRM's Blind Spots](/blog/krayin-crm-three-cves)

## Bug bounty

I test web targets on **HackerOne**, **Bugcrowd** and **Intigriti**. My focus is
server-side and access-control bugs rather than surface-level reflected issues:
IDOR / BOLA, broken authentication and authorization, SSRF, injection and
business-logic flaws.

## Focus areas

- **Access control** — IDOR, BOLA, privilege escalation, broken tenant isolation
- **Authentication** — session handling, JWT and OAuth flaws, SSO
- **Server-side** — SSRF, SQL injection, SSTI, deserialization
- **Logic & timing** — business-logic abuse and race conditions

Everything is handled through responsible disclosure: findings go to the vendor or
program first, and details are published only once a fix is available.
